90 x 65 mm Y-axis channel arrangement - 2026-11-18

Three channels retain the user's original amplifier/ADC orientation.
CH0, CH1 and CH2 have identical main-component X coordinates and a 21.40 mm Y pitch.
The three NJM2732 amplifiers and the ADS8331 in each channel retain the exact relative positions of the user-revised source block; no block rotation was applied.
Input filters, bypass capacitors, gain switches/resistors and connectors were adjusted where the Pico or mounting clearance required it. Repeated peripheral placement was used across channels wherever possible.
USB faces left; the three analog input connectors face right, ordered CH0/CH1/CH2 from top to bottom.

Board: 90.00 x 65.00 mm, 1.6 mm FR-4, four copper layers.
All 159 components are on the front.
Four 3.20 mm NPTH hole centres are exactly 5.00 mm from both adjacent edges; footprints and outline are locked.
Minimum track width: 0.20 mm. Copper clearance: 0.15 mm. Smallest via: 0.50 / 0.20 mm.

F.Cu: horizontal preferred for new routing.
In1.Cu: continuous GND plane only; one filled connected region.
In2.Cu: +5V and +3V3 power planes only; each a single filled connected region.
B.Cu: vertical preferred for new routing.
There are no signal tracks on either inner layer. Existing short local analog copper was retained where possible, including some 45-degree segments.
New routing prioritizes Manhattan distance and fewer layer transitions. Several longer runs were simplified after routing.
Vias: 241, down from 326 in the previous 90 x 50 mm revision (85 fewer, about 26%).
The ADC reference spines were shifted 0.80 mm inside the ADC bodies to make room for control-pin escapes.

Pico uses the project-local BoardParts:RPi_Pico_SMD_TH_Compact footprint, with its original through-hole and pin locations.
As in the previous compact revision, outer SMD land toes are 2.85 mm long rather than 3.50 mm.
The Pico module body has about 0.26 mm overhang at the USB-side board edge; all copper lands remain inside the board. The minimum copper-to-edge distance passes the 0.30 mm rule.
Schematic sheets and GPIO assignments were not changed in this revision.
